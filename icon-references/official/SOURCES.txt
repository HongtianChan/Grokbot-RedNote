Official robot icon references for Grokbot-RedNote.
Rendered as 512x512 PNGs in yellow (#F5C400) and red (#E03131) from official source SVGs.

Google Material Design Icons / Material Symbols (Apache License 2.0)
https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
- material-smart-toy-filled-*.png  (src/hardware/smart_toy materialicons)
- material-smart-toy-outlined-*.png  (symbols/web/smart_toy outlined)
- material-robot-2-filled-*.png  (symbols/web/robot_2 outlined fill)
- material-robot-2-outlined-*.png  (symbols/web/robot_2 outlined)

Lucide Icons "bot" (ISC)
https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide
- lucide-bot-*.png

Feather Icons and Heroicons were checked; they do not include a robot/bot glyph, so nothing was taken from those sets.
Apple SF Symbols were not exported: their license does not allow redistributing extracted symbol PNGs as standalone icon templates.
